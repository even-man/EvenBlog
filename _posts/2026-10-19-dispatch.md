What will you invariably see when you login to your local towns online group - be it Facebook, Nextdoor, or some other platform. Thats right, a post going "I just heard a police car go by! Anyone know whats going on?!".

Of course, no one actually knows what is going on, or at least the one person that does is drowned by the replies of people speculating.

Fear not, though, your questions shall soon be answered.

Using publicly available data from the City of Fayetteville, the [Dispatch Log Viewer](https://dispatch.stevenman.us) provides a useful interface for viewing local events in real time.

Try it at <a href="https://fayarpop.stevenman.us" target="_blank">[https://dispatch.stevenman.us](https://dispatch.stevenman.us)</a>
